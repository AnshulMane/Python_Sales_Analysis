## Sales Analysis Visuals

The following images represent answers to essential questions from the sales analysis:

1. **Best Month for Sales** (IMG_1.png)
   - **Question**: What was the best month for sales? How much was earned that month?
   - This visual identifies the month with the highest sales and the corresponding earnings, helping highlight which months perform the best.

   

2. **Top-Selling City** (IMG_2.png)
   - **Question**: What city sold the most products?
   - This chart shows the city with the highest product sales, allowing for better targeting in specific geographic areas.

  

3. **Optimal Advertisement Timing** (IMG_3.png)
   - **Question**: What time should we display advertisements to maximize the likelihood of customers buying the product?
   - This graph visualizes sales performance based on the time of day, helping identify the best times to display advertisements and boost product purchases.

  

4. **Top-Selling Product** (IMG_4.png)
   - **Question**: What product sold the most? Why do you think it sold the most?
   - This visual shows which product had the highest sales and provides insights into potential reasons for its success, such as demand or seasonal factors.
